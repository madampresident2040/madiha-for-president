What I'm Running On

Four Things I Want to Get Done

These aren't talking points — they're things I've either already started or know we need to build.

🌱
Stronger Community

Better programming, more consistent events, and real connections between MSA chapters — not just at conference time, but throughout the year.

📚
Resources for Students

Workshops, mentorship, and support that actually helps students figure out what they want to do — and how to get there.

💼
Career & Economic Access

More internship pipelines, career panels, and financial literacy resources. Muslim students deserve the same access as everyone else.

Top Priority
🔗
Finally Tap Our Alumni

We have alumni all over the country who went through Manara West and want to help. Right now, there's no real system for that. I want to build one — so students can actually reach the people who've been in their shoes.

If I'm Elected

Here's What I'll Actually Do

Specific, realistic, and grounded in what I've already been doing.

01
Build an Alumni Program

Create a real system for connecting alumni with current students — mentorship matching, career panels, and a network that stays active year-round, not just during events.

02
Grow Professional Development

More workshops, more speakers, more partnerships with organizations that can open doors for Muslim students. I've run these events — I know what works and what doesn't.

03
Expand Spiritual Programming

Take what we've built with qiyams in Sacramento and bring it to more cities. Faith and community shouldn't only happen once a year at conference.

04
Improve Our Digital Tools

I built the conference site. I built the Ramadan page. I know what our digital infrastructure looks like and where it falls short. I want to fix that — better tools for events, communication, and staying connected.
